Kerala Vegetable Stew

A pale, peppery coconut stew where vegetables stay distinct, sweet and tender—the perfect calm counterpoint to appam.

20 minprep25 mincook45 mintotalEasydifficultyMildspice

Base yield

4 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp coconut oil
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 4 cloves
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 inch ginger, sliced
  • 2 green chillies, slit
  • 2 potatoes, cubed
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 150 g green beans
  • 100 g peas
  • 250 ml thin coconut milk
  • 200 ml thick coconut milk
  • 8 curry leaves
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Make it

Method

01

Heat coconut oil over low-medium heat. Bloom cinnamon and cloves, then soften onion, ginger and chilli without browning.

02

Add potato, carrot, beans, peas, thin coconut milk, salt and pepper. Cover and simmer until the potato yields easily to a knife.15:00

03

Stir in thick coconut milk and curry leaves. Warm just to a gentle steam—do not boil after this point. Taste for salt and pepper.

04

Rest 5 minutes, then serve with appam, idiyappam or soft bread.

Notes

Swap vegetables freely with cauliflower, mushrooms or green peas. Keeps 3 days refrigerated; warm slowly. For a richer, less traditional stew, use all thick coconut milk.
