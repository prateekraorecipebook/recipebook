Thai Red Curry

A quick, high-colour coconut curry with tofu, crisp vegetables, basil and a little lime at the finish.

15 minprep20 mincook35 mintotalEasydifficultyMediumspice

Base yield

4 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 3 tbsp red curry paste
  • 400 ml coconut milk
  • 250 g firm tofu, cubed
  • 1 broccoli, small florets
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 150 g green beans
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp brown sugar
  • 1 lime
  • Thai basil, optional garnish
  • Steamed jasmine rice, to serve

Make it

Method

01

Warm oil in a wide pan over medium heat. Fry curry paste for 1 minute until fragrant and glossy.

02

Add half the coconut milk and stir until the paste dissolves. Let it bubble gently until a little oil separates at the edge.03:00

03

Add remaining coconut milk, tofu, broccoli, pepper and beans. Simmer until the broccoli is tender-crisp.08:00

04

Season with soy and sugar. Squeeze in half the lime; taste, then add more lime, salt or curry paste as needed. Scatter basil just before serving.

Notes

Use yellow curry paste for a gentler result. Keeps chilled for 3 days; reheat gently and loosen with water. For a gluten-free dish, choose tamari.
