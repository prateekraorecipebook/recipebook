Sri Lankan · condiment · vegan

Brinjal Moju

Glossy fried eggplant folded through a sharp-sweet mustard pickle dressing. A small spoonful wakes up an entire plate.

20 minprep30 mincook50 mintotalMediumdifficultyHotspice

Base yield

6 servings

Ingredients

  • 500 g eggplant, batons
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 250 ml oil, for shallow frying
  • 2 onions, thinly sliced
  • 4 green chillies, sliced
  • 10 curry leaves
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 0.5 tsp turmeric
  • 80 ml coconut or malt vinegar
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • Salt to taste

Make it

Method

01

Toss eggplant with salt and leave for 10 minutes. Pat very dry—this makes the frying safer and the eggplant less oily.

02

Shallow-fry in batches over medium-high heat until browned and collapsed. Drain on a rack or paper; reserve 1 tablespoon oil.12:00

03

In the reserved oil, fry onion, chilli and curry leaves until the onion is soft with golden edges. Add mustard and turmeric; cook 30 seconds.

04

Add vinegar, sugar, chilli flakes and salt. Bubble until the sugar dissolves, then fold in fried eggplant. Cool completely before serving so the flavours settle.

Notes

Use long, firm eggplants and avoid crowding the pan. Refrigerate in a clean sealed jar for up to 1 week; its flavour develops overnight. Serve beside rice and curry, or in a sandwich.
