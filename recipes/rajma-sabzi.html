Punjabi · main · vegetarian

Rajma Sabzi

Kidney beans simmered until creamy in a deep tomato-onion masala. Humble, hearty, and especially good with rice.

15 minprep40 mincook55 mintotalEasydifficultyMediumspice

Base yield

4 servings

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp oil or ghee
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 3 tomatoes, grated
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 0.5 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
  • 800 g cooked kidney beans, with liquid
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tbsp yogurt, optional
  • 2 tbsp coriander leaves, optional
  • Salt; rice or roti to serve

Make it

Method

01

Heat oil in a heavy pot. Let cumin sizzle, then cook onion with a pinch of salt until deeply golden—not merely soft.12:00

02

Stir in ginger-garlic paste for 1 minute. Add tomatoes, coriander, turmeric and chilli; fry until thick and the oil shows at the edges.

03

Add kidney beans and their liquid plus 150 ml water. Simmer uncovered, mashing a spoonful of beans against the pot for a creamy gravy.20:00

04

Finish with garam masala and optional yogurt. Rest 10 minutes; scatter coriander and serve with hot rice.

Notes

For dried beans, soak 250 g overnight then pressure-cook until completely soft. Keeps 4 days chilled and tastes better tomorrow; freeze for 2 months. Use coconut yogurt for vegan rajma.
